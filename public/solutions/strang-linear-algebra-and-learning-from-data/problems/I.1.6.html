If three corners of a parallelogram are  ⁣(1,1)\!\left( 1,1 \right)(1,1),  ⁣(4,2)\!\left( 4,2 \right)(4,2), and  ⁣(1,3)\!\left( 1,3 \right)(1,3), what are all three of the possible fourth corners? Draw two of them.

The other possible fourth corners are  ⁣(−2,2)\!\left( -2,2 \right)(−2,2),  ⁣(4,0)\!\left( 4,0 \right)(4,0), and  ⁣(4,4)\!\left( 4,4 \right)(4,4).
