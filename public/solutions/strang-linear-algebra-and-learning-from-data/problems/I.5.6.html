When a matrix is symmetric or orthogonal, it will have orthogonal eigenvectors. This is the most important source of orthogonal vectors in applied mathematics.

One intuition to motivate here is that the transpose of a permutation matrix takes the position of the 1’s and reflects them over the main diagonal. Therefore, PAPAPA permutes the rows of AAA, but PTPAP^{\textsc{T}}PAPTPA permutes them back to where they were. Therefore, PTPP^{\textsc{T}}PPTP. It immediately follows that P−1=PTP^{-1} = P^{\textsc{T}}P−1=PT. Thus PPP is orthogonal.
