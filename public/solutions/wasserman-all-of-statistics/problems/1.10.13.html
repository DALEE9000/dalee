Suppose that a fair coin is tossed repeatedly until both a head and tail have appeared at least once.

  1. Describe the sample space Ω\OmegaΩ.

  2. What is the probability that three tosses will be required?

(a) The sample space is given by Ω={HT,HHT,…,H⋯H⏟kT,…,TH,TTH,…,T⋯T⏟kH,… }\Omega = \{HT, HHT, \dots, \underbrace{H \cdots H}_{k}T, \dots, TH, TTH, \dots, \underbrace{T \cdots T}_{k}H, \dots \}Ω={HT,HHT,…,kH⋯H​​T,…,TH,TTH,…,kT⋯T​​H,…}

(b) The theoretical derivation of the probability is P(3 tosses)=P(HHT)+P(TTH)=(1/2)3+(1/2)3=1/4\begin{align*} \mathbb{P}(\text{3 tosses}) & = \mathbb{P}(HHT) + \mathbb{P}(TTH) \\ & = (1/2)^{3} + (1/2)^{3} \\ & = \boxed{1/4} \end{align*}P(3 tosses)​=P(HHT)+P(TTH)=(1/2)3+(1/2)3=1/4​​
