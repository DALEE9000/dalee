Suppose we toss a fair coin until we get exactly two heads. Describe the sample space SSS. What is the probability that exactly kkk tosses are required?

Descriptively, the sample space SSS is the set of events where the first head appears on any one of the 1,…,k−11,\dots, k-11,…,k−1-th toss. In general, the probability that exactly kkk tosses are needed is

P(k tosses)=(k−1)(1/2)k\mathbb{P}(k \text{ tosses}) = (k-1)(1/2)^{k}P(k tosses)=(k−1)(1/2)k The reasoning is that since the second head is fixed on the kkk-th toss, there are k−1k-1k−1 tosses for the first head to be flipped. We simulate the coin toss experiment below:
