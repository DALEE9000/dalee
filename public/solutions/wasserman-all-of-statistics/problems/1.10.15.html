The probability that a child has blue eyes is 1/41/41/4. Assume independence between children. Consider a family with 333 children.

  1. If it is known that at least one child has blue eyes, what is the probability that at least two children have blue eyes?

  2. If it is known that the youngest child has blue eyes, what is the probability that at least two children have blue eyes?

(a) The complement event to "at least one child has blue eyes" – call it AAA – is "no child has blue eyes." The probability that a child does not have blue eyes is 1−p=3/41 - p = 3/41−p=3/4. Then we have P(A)=1−(3/4)3=1−27/64=37/64\mathbb{P}(A) = 1 - (3/4)^{3} = 1 - 27/64 = 37/64P(A)=1−(3/4)3=1−27/64=37/64 The probability that "at least two children have blue eyes" – call this one BBB – is the sum of the mutually exclusive probabilities of either two or three children having blue eyes. This is given by P(B)=P(2 children+3 children)=(32)(1/4)2(3/4)+(1/4)3=9/64+1/64=10/64\begin{align*} \mathbb{P}(B) & = \mathbb{P}(\text{2 children} + \text{3 children}) \\ & = \binom{3}{2} (1/4)^{2} (3/4) + (1/4)^{3} \\ & = 9/64 + 1/64 \\ & = 10/64 \end{align*}P(B)​=P(2 children+3 children)=(23​)(1/4)2(3/4)+(1/4)3=9/64+1/64=10/64​ Now we can calculate the probability of at least two children having blue eyes, given that at least one child has blue eyes. P(B∣A)=P(BA)P(A)=(10/64)(64/37)=10/37\begin{align*} \mathbb{P}(B \mid A) & = \frac{\mathbb{P}(BA)}{\mathbb{P}(A)} \\ & = (10/64)(64/37) \\ & = \boxed{10/37} \end{align*}P(B∣A)​=P(A)P(BA)​=(10/64)(64/37)=10/37​​

(b) The probability that the youngest child has blue eyes (call it C) is 1/41/41/4. Let DDD be the event that at least two children have blue eyes, with one of them being the youngest child. Then we have P(D)=2(1/4)2(3/4)+(1/4)3=6/64+1/64=7/64\begin{align*} \mathbb{P}(D) & = 2(1/4)^{2}(3/4) + (1/4)^{3} \\ & = 6/64 + 1/64 \\ & = 7/64 \end{align*}P(D)​=2(1/4)2(3/4)+(1/4)3=6/64+1/64=7/64​ Lastly, the probability that at least two children have blue eyes given that the youngest child is known to have blue eyes is P(D∣C)=P(DC)P(C)=7/641/4=7/16\begin{align*} \mathbb{P}(D \mid C) & = \frac{\mathbb{P}(DC)}{\mathbb{P}(C)} \\ & = \frac{7/64}{1/4} \\ & = \boxed{7/16} \end{align*}P(D∣C)​=P(C)P(DC)​=1/47/64​=7/16​​
