Fundamentally, this is a superposition argument. The total flux is the sum of the energy transfer anti-parallel to the temperature gradient (conduction) and the “movement” of energy by the physical translation of the molecules (convection).

The addition of the cρuVc \rho u \pmb{V}cρuV term arises from the notion of “advecting” energy across space in each of the three spatial dimensions, in addition to the spontaneous energy transfer (i.e. heat) in the direction of the negative temperature gradient.
