Explain why f∗gf * gf∗g is the same as g∗fg * fg∗f (periodic or infinite convolution).

One intuitive explanation is to consider two sheets of paper, and overlapping one with the other. It does not matter which sheet is moved to overlap with the other; the overlapped area will be the same irrespective of that choice.

Mathematically, a simple variable substitution τ=x−y\tau = x - yτ=x−y allows one to switch between f∗gf * gf∗g or g∗fg * fg∗f. Additionally, convolution in function space corresponds to multiplication in frequency space, and since the product of coefficients commutes, it follows that convolution also commutes.
