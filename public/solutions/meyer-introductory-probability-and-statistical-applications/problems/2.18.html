One integer is chosen at random from the numbers 1, 2, ..., 50. What is the probability that the chosen number is divisible by 6 or by 8?

The numbers divisible by 6 are {6,12,18,24,30,36,42,48}\{ 6, 12, 18, 24, 30, 36, 42, 48 \}{6,12,18,24,30,36,42,48}, and by 8 are {8,16,24,32,40,48}\{ 8, 16, 24, 32, 40, 48 \}{8,16,24,32,40,48}. The only numbers divisible by both are 242424 and 484848.
