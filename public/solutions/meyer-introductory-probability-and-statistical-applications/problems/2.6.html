A lot consists of 10 good articles, 4 with minor defects, and 2 with major defects. One article is chosen at random. Find the probability that:

  1. it has no defects,

    Choose one of ten good items out of sixteen total items, (101)(161)=5/8\frac{\binom{10}{1} }{\binom{16}{1}} = \boxed{5/8}(116​)(110​)​=5/8​.

  2. it has no major defects,

    Choose one of ten good items or one of 4 minor defect items out of sixteen total items, (101)+(41)(161)=7/8\frac{\binom{10}{1} + \binom{4}{1} }{\binom{16}{1}} = \boxed{7/8}(116​)(110​)+(14​)​=7/8​.

  3. it is either good or has major defects.
