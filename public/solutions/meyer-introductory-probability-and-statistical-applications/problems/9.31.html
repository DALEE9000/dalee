The annual rainfall at a certain locality is known to be a normally distributed random variable with mean value equal to 29.5 inches and standard deviation 2.5 inches. How many inches of rain (annually) is exceeded about 5 percent of the time?

Equivalently, we solve for aaa, the threshold amount of annual rain exceeded 5 percent of the time, corresponding to the zzz-score

z=a−29.52.5z = \frac{a - 29.5}{2.5}z=2.5a−29.5​
