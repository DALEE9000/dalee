Two defective tubes get mixed up with two good ones. The tubes are tested, one by one, until both defectives are found.

Here, we need only apply the result of problem 2.21, namely that for rrr defects in a queue of nnn items, the probability of finding the last defective item in the kkk-th position is (k−1r−1)/(nr)\binom{k-1}{r-1} / \binom{n}{r}(r−1k−1​)/(rn​).
