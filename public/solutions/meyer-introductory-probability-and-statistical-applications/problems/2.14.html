From the letters a, b, c, d, e, and f how many 4-letter code words may be formed if,

  1. no letter may be repeated?

    Order matters, so this is a permutation problem. There are 6 ⁣P4=360{}^{6}\!P_{4} = \boxed{360}6P4​=360​ words.

  2. any letter may be repeated any number of times?

    64\boxed{6^4}64​ words.
