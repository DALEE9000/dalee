A certain type of electric motor fails either by seizure of the bearings, or by burning out of the electric windings, or by wearing out of the brushes. Suppose that seizure is twice as likely as burning out, which is four times as likely as brush wearout. What is the probability that failure will be by each of these three mechanisms?

Let P(brush wearout)=pP(\text{brush wearout}) = pP(brush wearout)=p. Then P(burning out)=4pP(\text{burning out}) = 4pP(burning out)=4p and P(seizure)=8pP(\text{seizure}) = 8pP(seizure)=8p. Assuming mutual exclusivity of each event, we must have 13p=113p = 113p=1 or p=1/13p = 1/13p=1/13.
