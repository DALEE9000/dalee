A product is assembled in three stages. At the first stage there are 5 assembly lines, at the second stage there are 4 assembly lines, and at the third stage there are 6 assembly lines. In how many different ways may the product be routed through the assembly process?

By the multiplicative principle, 5⋅4⋅6=1205 \cdot 4 \cdot 6 = \boxed{120}5⋅4⋅6=120​ ways.
