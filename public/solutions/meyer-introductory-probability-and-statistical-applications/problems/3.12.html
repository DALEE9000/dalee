A die is tossed, and independently, a card is chosen at random from a regular deck. What is the probability that:

  1. the die shows an even number and the card is from a red suit?

    The probability of an even die roll is 1/21/21/2 and drawing a red card, 1/21/21/2. Therefore, by independence, P(even ∩ red)=1/2⋅1/2=1/4P(\text{even } \cap \text{ red}) = 1/2 \cdot 1/2 = \boxed{1/4}P(even ∩ red)=1/2⋅1/2=1/4​.

  2. the die shows an even number or the card is from a red suit?

    Using the previous result, P(even ∪ red)=P(even)+P(red)−P(even ∩ red)=1/2+1/2−1/4=3/4P(\text{even } \cup \text{ red}) = P(\text{even}) + P(\text{red}) - P(\text{even } \cap \text{ red}) = 1/2 + 1/2 - 1/4 = \boxed{3/4}P(even ∪ red)=P(even)+P(red)−P(even ∩ red)=1/2+1/2−1/4=3/4​.
