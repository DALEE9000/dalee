It has been found that the number of transistor failures on an electronic computer in any one-hour period may be considered as a random variable having a Poisson distribution with parameter 0.1. (That is, on the average there is one transistor failure every 10 hours.) A certain computation requiring 20 hours of computing time is initiated.

  1. Find the probability that the above computation can be successfully completed without a breakdown. (Assume that the machine becomes inoperative only if 3 or more transistors fail.)

    Let XXX be the number of failures. By premise, the machine is inoperable if 3 or more transistors fail.
