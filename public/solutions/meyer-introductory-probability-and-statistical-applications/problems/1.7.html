  2. Suppose that the above bulbs are tested, one by one, until all defectives have been tested. Describe the sample space for this experiment.

    The sample space consists of sequences of every combination of 1,2,...,N−r1, 2, ..., N-r1,2,...,N−r non-defectives with the rrr defectives such that the rrr-th defective is the last of the sequence.
