  1. Solids. The lattice spacing in a typical solid is around d=0.3d = 0.3d=0.3 nm. Find the temperature below which the free3 electrons in a solid are quantum mechanical. Below what temperature are the nuclei in a solid quantum mechanical? (Use sodium as a typical case.) Moral: The free electrons in a solid are always quantum mechanical; the nuclei are almost never quantum mechanical. The same goes for liquids (for which the interatomic spacing is roughly the same), with the exception of helium below 4 K.
