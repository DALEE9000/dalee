Buffon’s needle. A needle of length lll is dropped at random onto a sheet of paper ruled with parallel lines a distance lll apart. What is the probability that the needle will cross a line? Hint: Refer to Problem 1.12.

Some intuition to motivate us:

  • Fundamentally, there are two independent variables: θ∈ ⁣[0,π]\theta \in \!\left[ 0,\pi \right]θ∈[0,π], the angle of the needle relative to the lines, and yyy, the vertical distance between the center of the needle to the nearest line. Assume both are distributed uniformly.

  • The likelihood that the needle will fall parallel and land on one of the paper’s lines is negligible.

  • The more vertical the needle is, the higher the likelihood it will intersect with one of the lines. More precisely, P ⁣(cross)↑P \!\left( \text{cross} \right) \uparrowP(cross)↑ when l∣cos⁡ ⁣(θ)∣↓l \left\lvert \cos\!\left(\theta\right)\right\rvert \downarrowl∣cos(θ)∣↓.
